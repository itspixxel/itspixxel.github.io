I’m **Sarthak Sachdeva**, or **Pixxel** online - a game developer and technical artist. I graduated from the University of Staffordshire with a **first-class B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Games Development**, and I now work full-time as a Game Developer at **AriseMedia**, building ability VFX, UI and asset pipelines for [Superhero Arena](/projects/superhero-arena/) on Roblox.

## I’ll probably build a tool for that

If I’m placing every pebble by hand, I’m already thinking about a scatter tool. Inspired by John Romero’s approach to tooling, I like building things that take repetitive work out of the development pipeline.

## What I work with

- **Unreal Engine 5:** Control Rig, Blueprints, Niagara, Geometry Script, materials and shaders
- **Code:** C++ and C#
- **Engines and content:** Unity, Roblox Studio (including EditableMesh), Blender, Substance Designer and Painter, Photoshop
- **Production:** profiling, technical documentation, asset integration, and handing tools over to artists and designers

## Outside work

These days, it’s Minecraft, Valorant, Overwatch, and usually another app idea. My current side goal: learning to build my own programming language.
