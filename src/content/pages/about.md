I’m Sarthak Sachdeva, a game developer and technical artist. I use the username **Pixxel** online.

## It started with a Discord bot

A friend was working on a Discord bot during a call, and I wanted to know how it worked. A few months of teaching myself later, I was building my own: a MyAnimeList-style tracking system, a Kakegurui-inspired casino bot, and an idle game inspired by Stardew Valley. Making something people could interact with was what hooked me on programming.

## From bots to games

That curiosity took me to Unity, which was confusing at first but eventually clicked. I decided to pursue game development and moved to the UK to study at the University of Staffordshire, graduating with first-class honours. Before the degree, I spent a few months getting comfortable with Unreal Engine; the projects in this portfolio came together during my time at university.

## I’ll probably build a tool for that

I’m a fan of John Romero’s emphasis on custom tools and an efficient development pipeline. If I catch myself placing every pebble in a scene by hand, I’m already thinking about a scatter or painting tool. I like putting time into something that makes the next hundred repetitions easier. Usually, it starts with “surely I don’t have to keep doing this manually.”

## Away from the editor

Minecraft, Valorant, and Overwatch are regular fixtures in my day. Outside games, I build apps and whatever other software idea has caught my attention. I find it difficult to sit still without something to make, even after a busy week at work. My current rabbit hole is learning how to build my own programming language.
