## The effect

Radiant Pulse is an energy beam inspired by the Kamehameha from Dragon Ball. The character charges up inside spinning golden rings, then fires a beam with a bright core and a softer purple-edged outer layer, kicking up dust around their feet.

## Tools

Unreal Engine 5 Niagara and materials, Photoshop.
