## The project

On The Way is a mobile puzzle game for young children, inspired by Mini Motorways and Teeny Tiny Trains. The player draws a road from start to finish, and a toy car follows it, collecting stars on the way.

I made it on my own in Unity. The published version is my fourth attempt at the idea.

## What I worked on

- **Drawing roads:** turning a finger drag into a road on the level grid, with large, forgiving touch targets for small hands.
- **Pathfinding:** A\* pathfinding so the car follows whatever road the player has drawn.
- **Levels and progression:** handcrafted levels, star collection, and a level select screen that builds itself as levels are added.
- **Saves:** cloud saves, so progress carries across devices.
