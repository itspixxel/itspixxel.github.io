## The idea

I made EMP Kunai after feedback on ADAPTIK at [GradEX](https://www.staffs.ac.uk/gradex) pushed me to show more of my problem-solving as a technical artist. It became an original personal study of a throwable EMP ability, using Niagara VFX and character animation to explore timing and visual feedback.
